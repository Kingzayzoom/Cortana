# Cortana

## Register

product

## Users

Healthcare professionals taking a brief learning break at a laptop in a bright workplace, seeking a calm, focused educational conversation.

## Product Purpose

A voice-first two-minute cardiology learning round: listen, question, practice with one synthetic case, inspect evidence, and save progress.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, luminous. The supplied Cortana visualization is the primary layout reference. The supplied ShaderGradient palette informs the live orb's blue, violet, and pink material.

## Anti-references

Generic AI chat dashboards, dark sci-fi consoles, fake audio visualizers, fabricated mastery scores, and unsupported patient-care claims.

## Design Principles

- Center the voice conversation and its procedural orb.
- Make state truthful and recovery straightforward.
- Ground one complete lesson in a small versioned source bundle.
- Preserve the reference's spacious white clinical workspace.
- Treat learning progress as practice history, not competence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, readable contrast, text alternatives to voice, explicit status, reduced motion, and a disclosed WebGL fallback.

Strategic context supplied explicitly in the user's complete implementation brief; no additional interview required.
